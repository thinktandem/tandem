After many incredible years of building digital experiences, solving complex technical challenges, and working with amazing clients, **Kalabox Inc. DBA Tandem** has closed its doors as of December 31st, 2025.

We're incredibly grateful for all the relationships we've built, the projects we've delivered, and the impact we've made together. From building tools like [Lando](https://lando.dev) to helping Fortune 500 companies migrate their platforms, it's been an honor to be part of your digital journeys.

## What's Next?

While Tandem's chapter has closed, our adventures continue! Here's where you can find us:

### Mike Pirog
Mike has started a new venture at **[Tanaab Maneuvering Systems LLC](https://www.tanaab.dev/)**, a tech workshop for special projects. You can reach out to him there for your next technical challenge.

### Alec Reynolds
Alec has launched **[Switchback Solutions](https://switchbacksolutions.net)**, continuing to help organizations navigate their digital transformations. Connect with him there to continue your digital journey.

### Lando

Both Mike and Alec are continuing to foster Lando as board members of the Lando Alliance, a 501c3 nonprofit organization. The Lando Alliance has assumed ownership over Lando's intellectual property, including the logo and lando.dev domains.

## Thank You

To all our clients, partners, contributors, and friends—thank you for being part of this incredible journey. We couldn't have done it without you.

Here's to new adventures and continued innovation!

— Mike & Alec
