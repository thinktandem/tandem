After many incredible years of building great things together, we're announcing that **Kalabox Inc. DBA Tandem** has closed its doors as of December 31st, 2025.

## A Thank You

We want to express our deepest gratitude to everyone who has been part of Tandem's journey:

- Our amazing clients who trusted us with their most important projects
- Our talented team members who brought passion and expertise to every challenge
- Our partners and collaborators who made the work possible

## What's Next

While Tandem's chapter has closed, our adventures continue:

### Mike Pirog

Mike has moved on to new ventures and can be found at **[Tanaab Maneuvering Systems LLC](https://www.tanaab.dev/)**. A tech workshop for special projects, Tanaab continues Mike's passion for building innovative solutions.

### Alec Reynolds

Alec is continuing his work in digital solutions and can be reached at **[Switchback Solutions](https://switchbacksolutions.net/)**. Switchback Solutions focuses on helping organizations navigate their digital transformation journeys.

### Lando

Both Mike and Alec are continuing to foster Lando as board members of the Lando Alliance, a 501c3 nonprofit organization. You can continue to follow Lando at https://lando.dev.

## Looking Back

From building popular open source tools like Lando and Kalabox, to helping Fortune 500 companies with their digital strategies, to supporting non-profits and higher education institutions—we're proud of the impact we've made together.

Thank you for being part of this journey. We look forward to seeing what the future holds.
